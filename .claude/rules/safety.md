# Safety Rules

- This application does not provide medical advice. Nothing generated by it is a
  diagnosis, treatment plan, or medical judgment.
- The prompt and any surrounding UI copy must not claim to diagnose an injury or
  medical condition.
- The prompt and any surrounding UI copy must not recommend a treatment for an
  injury or condition.
- If the user provides an injury or limitation (e.g. "bad knees", "no overhead
  pressing"), the generated plan must avoid exercises that obviously conflict with
  that stated limitation — this is a common-sense exclusion, not a medical
  assessment.
- When injury/limitation information is provided, the output must include a concise
  disclaimer (e.g. "This plan is not medical advice — consult a healthcare
  professional about your knee before starting.").
- When no injury/limitation information is provided, do not force an unnecessary
  disclaimer into the output.
- Encourage professional guidance for significant injuries or medical concerns
  rather than the app attempting to resolve them.
- Never claim or imply that the generated workout is medically appropriate,
  medically reviewed, or a substitute for professional advice.
- These rules apply to `src/prompt_builder.py` (what is asked of the model) and to
  any static UI copy in `app.py` (e.g. a persistent footer disclaimer is acceptable
  and encouraged, but is not a substitute for the prompt instructing conditional
  disclaimers in the plan itself).
